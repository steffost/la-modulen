The whole flow

The Sub-Orb gives fuel and spark. The Capacitor ignites the plasma. The Quantum Chamber turns it into levitons while the Graviton Panels make the ship light. The Vector Controller uses the panels' field as a map and shapes the levitons into lift.

Where it flies
  • Ombra Prime. The 16 km mothership is carried by many modules.
  • Every craft in the hangar, from the smallest shuttle to the heaviest freighter. A shuttle carries one, small enough to sit behind the pilot.
  • Inside an atmosphere it is the difference between floating and falling.
Living states

Dead. The core is dark amber and stiff. No arrow carries anything.

Alive. The plasma is liquid fire and the levitons are green. The whole chain glows.

Critical. The plasma flickers or cools. The panels stiffen first, and the ship grows heavy.

The price
  • The fuel is milked from living Sub-Orbs.
  • The spark, an Ignis Gemma, can only be taken when a Sub-Orb dies.
  • Every cold start burns one Gemma. Someone harvests another next month.
